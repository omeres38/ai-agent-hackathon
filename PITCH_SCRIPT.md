# AgenticPay pitch video script (up to 2 minutes)

“Hi, I’m Ömer, the founder of AgenticPay.

AI agents are becoming capable of taking actions, but payments are still designed primarily for humans. Giving an agent unrestricted wallet access is risky, while requiring a human to approve every small transaction creates friction.

AgenticPay explores a programmable payment-control layer for autonomous AI agents. The owner defines a daily spending limit, a maximum payment amount, and recipient permissions. When an agent requests a payment, the policy layer evaluates those rules and explains why the request is approved or rejected.

Our current browser prototype demonstrates this decision flow with simulated payment requests and a local history. It does not yet connect a wallet or execute on-chain transactions.

We propose Solana and USDC as a first settlement path for a future version. The next milestones are automated policy tests, a server-side policy API, and a devnet-only transaction adapter before any production use.

Our long-term goal is to let agents purchase data, APIs, compute, software, and services autonomously—without giving them unrestricted access to money.

AgenticPay is an early-stage prototype, but we believe safe spending controls can become essential infrastructure for the agent economy. Thank you.”
